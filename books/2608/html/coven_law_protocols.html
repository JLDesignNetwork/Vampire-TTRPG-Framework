Coven Law & Protocols

Overview & System-Agnostic Adaptation

The Coven Law Court Protocols & Legal Code is an official, opt-in supplement for the Vampire TTRPG Framework. It codifies the judicial proceedings, tribunal trials, legal dispensations, and punishment enforcement governing vampiric society.

Following Separation of Concerns, this supplement expands upon the core Social & Coven Laws. All tribunal rulings consume standard Coven Governance Laws and utilize Universal Age Tier Superiority Modifiers.

Judicial Tribunal Architecture

The Presiding Magistrate & Tribunal Bench

Coven judicial trials are presided over by a panel of 3 Elder or Ancient Magistrates:

  • The High Arbiter: Presides over trial decorum, reads formal charges, and delivers final sentences.
  • The Sanguine Auditor: Examines physical evidence, Blood Archive phials, and vitae purity tests.
  • The Keeper of the Shroud: Evaluates secrecy breaches, mortal exposure risks, and diplomatic impact.

Evidence Admissibility & Blood Testimony

Vampiric courts enforce strict evidentiary standards:

  • Blood Archive Phials (Admissible): Uncorrupted memory phials extracted via Corpse Siphon or consentual blood extraction serve as absolute legal proof.
  • Telepathic Probe Logs (Conditional): Memories extracted via Mind Tear require verification by the Sanguine Auditor to rule out cognitive forgery or illusion tampering.
  • Mortal Testimony (Inadmissible): Mundane mortal testimony is legally invalid unless supported by a Thrall blood bond oath or memory phial.

The Four Stages of Coven Trial Procedure

  1. The Formal Indictment: The High Arbiter reads the charges alleging a violation of Coven Law (The Shroud, Haven Sanctuary, or Diablerie Taboo).
  2. Evidence Examination: The Sanguine Auditor inspects memory phials, crime scene soil, or physical remains.
  3. The Blood Oath Defense: The accused (or their designated Advocate) presents defense arguments, alibis, or Bloodline Alignment Bonuses.
  4. Verdict & Sentencing: The Tribunal Bench casts secret votes using obsidian (guilty) or white quartz (innocent) stones. Sentences are executed immediately.
"Three stones of obsidian, and it's over before dawn. That's the mercy of it — the Coven doesn't let you wait."

Not every sentence ends in ash. Termination, release, and treaty — the three formal dispensations a Coven Council can grant instead of a killing.

Legal Dispensations & Sanction Protocols

Sanctioned Termination Protocol

Under Coven Law, executing a fellow vampire or a bound Thrall without legal authorization is treated as murder or destruction of Coven property.

  • Execution Authorization: A Master seeking to terminate an overage Thrall or execute a rogue fledgling must receive a formal Sanctioned Termination Decree.
  • Grounds Restriction: Executions MUST be conducted strictly outside Coven sanctum grounds (to prevent desecration of sacred havens and avoid Native Soil contamination).

Sanctioned Release Dispensation

Resolving Thrall Cap Overage Strain without execution requires a formal Sanctioned Release Dispensation signed by the Coven Council:

  • Controlled Euthanasia: The Thrall is peacefully euthanized via painless alchemical narcotics or sedatives.
  • Memory Erasure & Discharge: The vampire uses Lethe's Touch under Tribunal supervision to completely erase all memories of vampiric society, releasing the mortal back into human society with a substantial financial severance.

Blood Treaty Diplomatic Accords

Formal agreements between rival Covens governing hunting perimeters, sanctuary boundaries, and extradition:

  • Sanctuary Immunity: A vampire seeking asylum inside a neutral Haven cannot be seized while diplomatic negotiations are underway.
  • Extradition Rights: Covens bound by a Blood Treaty must surrender fleeing lawbreakers within 72 hours of receiving a formal extradition writ.

Sanguine tithes, staked slumber, and sun fire — the three tiers of consequence that keep a Coven honest, arranged from a fine to an ending.

Penalties & Punishment Codex

Tier 1 Infractions (Sanguine Tithe & Fine)

Offenses: Minor Haven protocol breaches, unauthorized feeding in secondary zones, minor decorum disrespect.

Penalty: Offender must surrender 2 to 5 Blood Reserve phials into the Coven Vaults or serve 30 days of sanctuary guard duty.

Tier 2 Infractions (Enforced Torpor Slumber)

Offenses: Reckless secrecy breaches, assault on a Coven member, unauthorized Thrall creation.

Penalty: The offender is staked through the heart and interred in a sealed stone crypt for a period of 1 to 100 years of enforced Torpor Slumber.

Tier 3 Infractions (Tabula Rasa & Sun Fire Execution)

Offenses: Unsanctioned Diablerie, mass Shroud collapse, treasonous collaboration with vampire hunters.

Penalty:

  • Tabula Rasa: Complete psychic erasure of all memories, skills, and identity via high-level mental sorcery.
  • Sun Fire Execution: The offender is bound to a solar pillar at dawn, suffering total solar combustion down to ash.

The last word: this codex plugs directly into the core framework's governance and Thrall rules, with nothing left to reconcile.
