Bloodline Magic Compendium

The Bloodline Magic Compendium is an official, opt-in supplement for the Vampire TTRPG Framework. It expands vampiric blood powers into structured sorcery incantations, sacrificial rituals, and ancestral bloodline rites.

Following Separation of Concerns, this supplement does not alter core ruleset mechanics. All rituals consume standard Blood Reserves and scale using Universal Age Tier Superiority Modifiers.

Ritual Action Economy & Casting Windows

Unlike instantaneous Activated Powers cast during combat turns, Blood Rituals are complex incantations requiring extended concentration:

  • Tier 1 Rituals: Require 1 Minute (10 Combat Rounds) of continuous chanting and blood tracing.
  • Tier 2 Rituals: Require 10 Minutes of unbroken concentration.
  • Tier 3 Primordial Rites: Require 1 Hour (60 Minutes) of ritual channeling and coven alignment.
  • No Pause & Resume: Blood rituals require continuous, unbroken channeling of vitae. A ritual cannot be paused and resumed under any circumstances.
  • Voluntary Cancellation (50% Refund): If a caster voluntarily aborts a ritual prior to completion, 50% of the spent Blood Reserves are forfeited (dissipated into ambient space), while the remaining 50% are retained in the caster's reservoir. Restarting an aborted ritual requires starting a fresh full-duration casting and paying the full Blood Reserve cost.

Blood Reserve Cost & Sacrificial Upkeep

Casting Cost: Rituals expend stored Blood Reserves upon initiation (Tier 1 = 1 Reserve; Tier 2 = 2 Reserves; Tier 3 = 3 Reserves).

Sacrificial Substitution: A caster may substitute stored Blood Reserves by draining a living creature during the ritual. Blood source eligibility and potency vary by type:

Sacrificial Blood Source Eligibility
Blood Source Volume Required per Reserve Notes
Living mortal / Thrall 250ml Standard potency. Full living vitae resonance.
Vampire vitae 100ml Concentrated potency. Donor must consent or be restrained. Counts as partial feeding — subject to Coven feeding laws.

Synth-Vitae phials 1 full pouch (500ml) Reduced potency. Lacks living essence; accepted by blood magic but at half efficiency.

Coven Circle Chants & Age Tier Synergy

  • Coven Circle Timing & Upkeep: Coven Circle Chants require a 1-minute pre-ritual alignment phase to establish the circle. Assisting secondary casters must remain inside the circle and continue chanting throughout the entire casting window to sustain circle bonuses. If a secondary member moves away or is disrupted, their contribution immediately ends.
  • Circle Ritual Bonuses: Each secondary vampire in the circle grants:
    • Casting Time Reduction: -10% casting time per secondary member (max 50% reduction).
    • Concentration Shielding: +1 bonus per secondary member to the Lead Caster's Concentration tests if disturbed during casting.
    • DC / Potency Stacking: +1 Coven Stacking Bonus per secondary member to the ritual's save DC or spell check (governed by Coven Power Ritual Nexus).
  • Lead Caster Tier Requirements: Secondary casters of any Age Tier may assist in any circle, but the Lead Caster initiating the ritual MUST meet the minimum Age Tier requirement for the arcana tier:
    • Tier 1 Neonate Arcana: Lead Caster must be Neonate (<500 yrs) or higher.
    • Tier 2 Elder Arcana: Lead Caster MUST be an Elder (500–1,199 yrs) or Ancient.
    • Tier 3 Primordial Rites: Lead Caster MUST be an Ancient (1,200+ yrs).
  • Age Tier Scaling: Ritual save DCs and potency scale using Age Tier Superiority Math (Δ): Neonate = Base DC; Elder = +1 DC / +1 Area; Ancient = +2 DC / Double Duration.

Ritual Interruption & Backfire Trauma

If a ritual caster suffers physical damage, knockback, or psychic attack during casting:

  • Concentration Test: The caster MUST pass a Self-Control check with difficulty scaled by Age Tier:
    • Neonate (< 500 yrs): DC 15 (Hard)
    • Elder (500–1,199 yrs): DC 12 (Medium)
    • Ancient (1,200+ yrs): DC 9 (Easy)
  • Backfire Trauma: On failure, the ritual collapses violently: spent Blood Reserves are lost, and the caster suffers 1 Direct Core Health Damage from uncontained blood backfire.

  • Defender Victory: The blood pool clouds with static, blocking the vision. The defender senses the probe and receives a psychic trace of the caster's general cardinal direction.

Ritual of the Crimson Ward

Tier 1 Neonate Arcana · Hemomancy
Cost1 Reserve
Duration24 Hours
Thresholds1–7

Power Amplification: Amplifies protection and repulsion powers (Bane-Blindness, Anointed Flesh), projecting bloodline defensive tethers onto physical architectural entryways.

Standalone Benefit (All Casters): The caster paints blood sigils across a threshold. Any uninvited mortal or rival vampire attempting to cross the threshold triggers a sudden searing blood burn, inflicting 1 Fire/Thermal Damage and sounding a psychic alarm to the caster.

Power Synergy Bonus: Casters who natively possess protection or defensive powers (Anointed Flesh or Alchemical Assimilation) receive a +2 Power Synergy Bonus to the ward's blood burn damage (inflicting 3 Fire/Thermal Damage total).

Age Tier Boundary Caps (maximum thresholds warded in a single casting):

  • Neonate Caster (< 500 yrs): 1 Threshold max (single doorway or window frame)
  • Elder Caster (500–1,199 yrs): Up to 3 Thresholds max within the same haven
  • Ancient Caster (1,200+ yrs): Up to 7 Thresholds max within the same haven

Invitation Protocol: The caster is always permanently self-invited to their own warded threshold. Additional guests may be verbally designated as invited at any time during the ward's active 24-hour duration. Invitations are revoked by the caster's explicit verbal or written decree, or automatically upon the caster's entry into Torpor or True Death. Exile Edge-Case: A Coven Council exile ruling does not override the caster's personal invitation authority over their own private haven ward — however, a Coven-sanctioned forced entry override may be requested as a formal Tribunal dispensation (see Coven Law Court Protocols).

"Uninvited guests learn the hard way that our doors bite back."

Vitae Purification Filter

Tier 1 Neonate Arcana · Hemomancy
Cost1 / 2 Reserve
DurationInstant
Volume1 Draft Max

Cost: 1 Reserve (Pre-Ingestion) / 2 Reserves (Emergency Post-Ingestion Purge).

Power Amplification: Amplifies internal blood control powers (Gangrene Touch, Grand Harmonization), projecting biological blood purity magic onto external or ingested blood.

Standalone Benefit (All Casters): Drawing a draft (~250ml) of harvested blood through palms instantly neutralizes all alchemical poisons, holy water taints, synthetic neurotoxins, or Reaper virus contagions present in that draft for 1 Reserve.

Power Synergy Bonus: Casters who natively possess blood control powers (Gangrene Touch or Grand Harmonization) receive a +2 Power Synergy Bonus to all Fortitude/Constitution checks against lingering systemic toxins or contagions.

Defined Caps & Boundaries:

  • Volume Cap: Pre-ingestion purification is capped at 1 draft (~250ml) per casting.
  • Emergency Purge Cap: Emergency post-ingestion purge (2 Reserves) must occur within 60 seconds of ingestion and is capped at max 1 Emergency Purge per Scene.

Note: Silver is omitted as it poses no supernatural toxicity to vampires; see Silver (Mythic Misconception).

"Drink first, filter second, ask questions never."

Rhythm of the Ancestral Lineage

Tier 2 Elder Arcana · Hemomancy
Cost2 Reserves
Duration1 Scene
Progeny1–7

Power Amplification: Amplifies Ancestral Memory. By burning blood in an ancient censer or bio-centrifuge, the caster breaks the core Historical Scope Lock of Ancestral Memory, opening a live real-time telepathic communion feed across the blood tether between Sire and progeny.

Standalone Benefit (All Casters): Burning the blood censer grants a +2 bonus to Cognition, Perception, and Historical Lore checks for 1 Scene to the caster and all allied bloodline members in the room (whether they possess Ancestral Memory or not).

Power Synergy Bonus: Casters who natively possess Ancestral Memory receive a +2 Power Synergy Bonus to all Bloodline Duel checks during live communion.

Age Tier Progeny Capacity Cap (number of bloodline progeny a Sire can monitor simultaneously): Neonate Caster = 1 Target max; Elder Caster = Up to 3 Progeny max; Ancient Caster = Up to 7 Progeny max.

Bloodline Duel & Detection Limits: The caster rolls 1 Bloodline Duel check against target(s):

  • Sire Probing Downward: Progeny do not automatically notice the probe — requires a Perception check (DC 14) to detect. Detected progeny roll Self-Control against the caster's result to sever their link.
  • Progeny Probing Upward: Higher-tier ancestors automatically detect the probe instantly. If the ancestor wins the Bloodline Duel, they sever the link, inflict 1 Direct Core Health Damage (psychic feedback) on the caster, and collapse the ritual.

Torpor/Deceased Cap: Probing a Sire or progeny in Torpor or True Death hard-caps back to baseline past memories only, adhering strictly to Ancestral Memory scope.

"Every Sire hears their line hum. Some just choose not to listen."

Hematic Curse of Inertia

Tier 2 Elder Arcana · Hemomancy
Cost2 Reserves
Duration1 Scene
Targets1–3

Power Amplification: Amplifies thermal and decay powers (Grave Rot), projecting cryogenic blood thickening magic into an enemy's circulatory system.

Standalone Benefit (All Casters): Target enemy within line of sight has their blood thickened to cold sludge. Movement speed is halved (0.5x speed), and Initiative is reduced by -2 for 1 Scene. Target may attempt a Hard Fortitude check at the start of each turn to break the curse.

Power Synergy Bonus: Casters who natively possess decay/necrotic powers (Grave Rot) receive a +2 Power Synergy Bonus to the curse's Fortitude resistance DC.

Age Tier Target Caps (maximum targets cursed in a single casting):

  • Neonate Caster (< 500 yrs): 1 Target max
  • Elder Caster (500–1,199 yrs): Up to 2 Targets max within line of sight
  • Ancient Caster (1,200+ yrs): Up to 3 Targets max within line of sight

Caster Dependence & Early Termination: The curse requires ongoing metaphysical tethering. If the caster is incapacitated, knocked unconscious, plunged into Torpor, or perishes, the curse immediately collapses and all targets' speed and Initiative are restored.

"Their blood forgets how to hurry. So do they."

Sanguine Transfusion Lock

Tier 2 Elder Arcana · Hemomancy
Cost2 Reserves
Duration1 Round
RangeExposed Vitae

Power Amplification: Amplifies blood-arresting and gravimetric powers (Grav-Crush), manipulating exposed external blood flow to freeze an enemy's motor nervous system.

Standalone Benefit (All Casters): Target wounded enemy bleeding exposed vitae has their blood flow violently locked in place. Forces target to drop held weapons and locks physical limbs for 1 combat round.

Power Synergy Bonus: Casters who natively possess blood control powers (Gangrene Touch or Grand Harmonization) receive a +2 Power Synergy Bonus to the lock check / Fortitude resistance DC.

Physical Lock Scope: While locked, the target cannot perform physical movement, attacks, or gesture-based casting, but retains full consciousness and may speak or invoke purely mental/telepathic powers (Mind Tear).

Stunlock Immunity Cap: A target affected by Transfusion Lock gains temporary vascular immunity and cannot be targeted by Sanguine Transfusion Lock again for the remainder of the Scene (preventing continuous stunlock loops).

"The blood stops. The panic doesn't."

Grand Rite of Hematic Transmutation

Tier 3 Primordial Rite · Hemomancy
Cost3 Reserves
Duration24 Hours
Volume1,000 Gal Max

Power Amplification: Amplifies alchemical blood powers (Gangrene Touch, Grand Harmonization), projecting biological transmutation magic across macro environmental water reservoirs.

Standalone Benefit (All Casters): Conducts a 1-hour ritual over a body of water (well, fountain, reservoir), transmuting up to 1,000 gallons into dark, blood-infused vitae. Vampires drinking it regenerate 1 Reserve per gallon (subject to Regeneration Cap). Mortals drinking it gain the Susceptibility condition (50% easier to enthrall for 24 hours).

Power Synergy Bonus: Casters who natively possess alchemical blood powers (Gangrene Touch or Grand Harmonization) receive a +2 Power Synergy Bonus to the 1-hour ritual completion check and a +2 bonus to Concentration tests if disturbed during casting.

Defined Caps & Boundaries:

  • Volume Cap: Transmutes up to 1,000 Gallons max per casting.
  • Duration Cap: Transmutation lasts strictly 24 hours before all remaining water reverts to its natural state. Thrall bonds established during the window remain in effect post-reversion.
  • Regeneration Cap: A vampire cannot regenerate more Blood Reserves from transmuted water in a single feeding session than their standard per-night Reserve pool cap (see Blood Reserve Math).

Thrall Susceptibility Protocol: Mortals drinking the transmuted water gain Susceptibility (50% easier to enthrall via deliberate blood bond feeding). Drinking transmuted water does NOT automatically create a Thrall bond — an intentional feeding enthrallment is still required. Standard Thrall Cap rules and overage penalties apply.

"The well remembers what it carried, long after it runs clear again."

Glossary & Terminology

Arcana Tiers: The three structured power grades of blood sorcery: Tier 1 (Neonate Rites), Tier 2 (Elder Sorcery), and Tier 3 (Primordial Rites).

Backfire Trauma: Lethal biological and mental backlash (Direct Core Health Damage) suffered on a critical failure during high-tier ritual casting.

Bloodline Conduit: A living blood descendant or sire link used to extend ritual ranges across vast physical distances.

Coven Circle Chant: Multi-caster cooperative incantation that reduces individual Blood Reserve costs and amplifies casting potency.

Hematic Anchor: A physical focus or prepared circle that anchors sustained ritual enchantments to a physical territory.

Incantation Action Economy: Standard combat action distribution requiring continuous concentration actions across consecutive rounds.

Primordial Rite: Tier 3 cataclysmic blood magic requiring multiple casters, ancient blood, and extended casting rituals.

Sacrificial Upkeep: Blood Reserves or mortal life essence required at regular intervals to sustain an active ritual ward.

Sanguine Scrying: Divinatory Hemomancy using reflective pools of vitae to perceive remote locations or track bloodlines.

Transmutation Window: The operational lifespan (e.g. 24 hours) during which alchemical blood transmutations remain stable.
