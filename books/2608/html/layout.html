Vampire TTRPG Framework Foreword

Foreword

This framework began as a question: what does it cost to live forever? Every rule in these pages traces back to that question — the hunger, the sunlight, the soil you can never truly leave behind.

"We did not set out to make monsters sympathetic. We set out to make them accountable."

Read these rules as a starting point, not a cage. Bend them to the story your table wants to tell.

Vampire TTRPG Framework

The Predatory Aura

Every vampire radiates an uncanny stillness that mortals register as instinctive dread before they consciously notice anything is wrong. This aura sharpens with age and hunger, and can be consciously damped — but never extinguished.

The Predator's Edge

Mortals within 30 feet suffer a passive -1 penalty to Perception checks against the vampire unless actively warned. The penalty increases to -2 if the vampire is at Hunger 3 or higher.

Territorial Boundaries

Within their established haven, a vampire gains advantage on all awareness checks. Crossing an uninvited vampire's threshold requires a contested Willpower check (DC 14).

Death is not the end of the process — it is the first of three thresholds. Few survive the crossing intact, and fewer still are permitted to attempt it twice.

Vampire TTRPG Framework

The Three Phases

Turning a mortal unfolds across the Death Threshold, the Sanguine Transfusion, and the First Awakening. Each phase can fail independently, and failure is rarely survivable for the mortal involved.

Phase Failure Consequences

A mortal who dies during Phase 1 does not turn — they simply die. A mortal whose heart stops during Phase 2 rises as an uncontrollable Wight. Only Phase 3 produces a stable vampire.

What a vampire was before still shapes what it is after. And even the undead have a death they can suffer twice — dismemberment, decapitation, and the slow pull of soil they can never fully leave.

Vampire TTRPG Framework

The Native Soil Dependency

Every vampire remains bound to the soil of their first death. Extended separation triggers the Home Sickness Regression Track, colloquially known among covens as the Bleed.

The Bleed Progression

Stage 1 (Day 7): -1 to all Blood pool rolls. Stage 2 (Day 14): Regeneration ceases. Stage 3 (Day 21): Torpor resistance DC doubles. Stage 4 (Day 28): Irreversible decay begins.

The numbers behind the hunger. Draining, feeding, blood quality, and the action economy that governs every turn spent fighting for another night.

Draining & Feeding Mechanics

Blood is life, resource, and weapon. Managing the Blood Pool requires careful balance between indulgence and restraint — overfeeding draws mortal attention; starving risks the Frenzy.

Feeding Action Economy

Draining 1 Blood point from an immobilized target takes 1 full round. In combat, an un-grappled target may contest each turn with an opposed Strength check.

Blood, bone, fire, and shadow answer to those who survive long enough to command them. The -mancy taxonomy catalogs every discipline a coven has confirmed exists.

Hemomancy: The Blood Disciplines

Hemomancy commands the lifeblood itself — boiling an enemy's veins from across the room, shaping hardened crimson blades, or sensing the pulse of living creatures through solid walls.

Blood Blade [Supernatural]

Cost: 2 Blood • Range: Self • Duration: 1 Scene

Expels a blade of hardened, crystallized vitae from the palm. Deals lethal damage (Str + 2) and bypasses mundane body armor.

Rite of the Crimson Ward

Requires: 3 Casters • Duration: Until Sunset

Anoints all thresholds of a structure with consecrated vitae. Uninvited vampires who cross suffer 1 un-soakable aggravated damage per turn within.

Vampire TTRPG Framework Glossary

Glossary

Awakening: The moment a turned mortal completes the Three Phases and rises as a vampire.

The Bleed: The regression suffered by a vampire separated too long from their native soil.

Blood Quality Tier: A classification of how potent a feeding source's blood is, from Common to Ancient.

Coven: A self-governing group of vampires bound by shared law and territory.

Hunger: The core drive stat governing a vampire's need to feed and its influence on combat rolls.

-mancy Taxonomy: The eight confirmed root disciplines of vampiric supernatural power.

Native Soil: The ground in which a vampire first died; prolonged separation triggers the Bleed.

Siring: The act of turning a mortal into a vampire.

Vampire TTRPG Framework Arsenal & Artifacts

Arsenal & Artifacts

Relics, consecrated arms, and alchemical apparatus recovered from ancient caches or engineered in clandestine coven labs.

Ash-Forged Dagger [Artifact • Tier III]

Weight: 1.2 kg • Attunement: 1 Night • Damage: Str + 2 Lethal

Forged from the compressed ash of an executed Elder. Wounds inflicted by this blade cannot be regenerated with Blood points for 24 hours.

"The steel remembers the fire that made it — and the blood that put the fire out."

Urn of the First Soil [Relic • Unique]

Weight: 3.5 kg • Charges: 3/3 • Recharge: Sunset at origin point

Carries the uncorrupted earth of the bearer's sire-ground. Expending 1 charge suspends the Bleed regression track for 7 days regardless of physical distance.

"A handful of home, bought with three centuries of exile."

Vampire TTRPG Framework Hemomancy Grimoire

Hemomancy Grimoire

Collected ritual formulae and spell cards transcribed from the Third Coven Archive.

Sanguine Siphon [Rite • Level 2]

Casting Time: 1 Action • Cost: 1 Blood • Range: Touch

Draws life essence directly through bare skin contact. The target suffers 2 un-soakable Lethal damage; the caster recovers 1 Blood point immediately.

"Skin to skin. Pulse to pulse. What is yours becomes mine."

Veil of the Crimson Mist [Rite • Level 3]

Casting Time: 2 Rounds • Cost: 3 Blood • Area: 20 ft radius

Exhales a dense, iron-scented fog that heavily obscures vision (Total Concealment). The caster and their bonded thralls see through the mist unimpeded.

"They search for a monster in the dark, forgetting the dark has teeth."

Blood-Mirror Scrying [Rite • Level 4]

Casting Time: 10 Minutes • Cost: 2 Blood • Duration: Concentration

Pours vitae across a silver or glass surface to perceive a location known to the caster as though physically present. Target may contest with Wits + Occult (DC 15) to sense the intrusion.

"The blood remembers every room it ever filled."

Heart-Lock Incantation [Rite • Level 5]

Casting Time: 1 Action • Cost: 4 Blood • Range: 30 ft

Commands the target's cardiac muscle to seize. Target is Paralyzed (contested Stamina check, DC 16, at the start of each turn to break free). Undead targets are Action-Locked for 1 round instead.

"Stop."

Corpse-Lantern Ward [Rite • Level 1]

Casting Time: 1 Minute • Cost: 1 Blood • Duration: Until Sunrise

Ignites a drop of vitae into a cold, shadowless violet flame. Reveals invisible creatures and magical residue within 15 ft; repels minor scavengers and vermin.

"A light that casts no shadow — because nothing living cast it."

Blood-Bond Seal [Rite • Level 3]

Casting Time: 1 Hour • Cost: 2 Blood (each participant) • Duration: 1 Year

Binds two willing participants in mutual blood-oath. Neither can knowingly speak falsehood to the other, and each senses the other's physical distress across any distance on the same plane.

"Two veins, one pulse."

Sanguine Armor [Rite • Level 2]

Casting Time: 1 Action • Cost: 2 Blood • Duration: 1 Scene

Hardens the outer epidermal layer with crystallized blood platelets, granting +2 to all Damage Soak rolls against mundane weapons (Bashing and Lethal).

"Let them strike. It is only blood meeting steel — and blood has all night."

Grave-Call Whispers [Rite • Level 2]

Casting Time: 5 Minutes • Cost: 1 Blood • Range: Touch (Corpse)

Draws residual memory echoes from a corpse dead no longer than 24 hours. The caster may ask 3 questions; the corpse answers in cryptic fragments reflecting its final moments of consciousness.

"The dead are terrible gossips — if you know which tongue to listen with."

Crimson Brand [Rite • Level 1]

Casting Time: 1 Action • Cost: 1 Blood • Range: Touch

Marks an object or willing subject with an invisible vitae sigil. The caster always knows the exact distance and direction to the mark up to 10 miles away.

"You can run as far into the daylight as you like. Night always follows."

Aegis of the Old Blood [Rite • Level 5]

Casting Time: 1 Full Round • Cost: 5 Blood • Duration: 3 Rounds

Envelops the caster in a shimmering crimson barrier. Completely absorbs up to 15 points of incoming damage (including Aggravated and Solar) before shattering.

"The blood of the ancients does not ask permission to endure. It simply refuses to yield."

Vital Essence Extraction [Rite • Level 4]

Casting Time: 10 Minutes • Cost: 1 Blood • Yield: 1 Phial of Condensed Vitae

Distills raw mortal blood into a stable alchemical elixir. Can be stored indefinitely in glass without coagulating; restores 2 Blood points upon consumption.

"Harvest the season before winter takes the vine."

Shadow-Thread Binding [Rite • Level 3]

Casting Time: 1 Action • Cost: 2 Blood • Range: 20 ft

Weaves tendrils of coagulated shadow around a target's shadow on the floor, pinning them in place (Movement speed reduced to 0; contested Dex check DC 14 to escape).

"When your own shadow betrays you, nowhere left is safe."

Vitae Transfusion Anchor [Rite • Level 3]

Casting Time: 1 Action • Cost: 2 Blood • Range: 30 ft

Establishes a temporary vitality tether between the caster and an ally. The caster may transfer up to 3 Blood points per turn across the tether as a Free Action.

"We fight as a coven, or we starve as individuals."

Alchemical Coagulant Ward [Rite • Level 2]

Casting Time: 1 Minute • Cost: 1 Blood • Duration: 1 Scene

Coats an edged weapon in alchemically treated vitae. The next successful strike forces a mortal target to begin bleeding out (1 lethal damage per round until treated).

"The cut is minor. It is the waiting that ends you."

Shroud of the Unseen Predator [Rite • Level 4]

Casting Time: 1 Action • Cost: 3 Blood • Duration: Concentration (up to 10 min)

Bends ambient light and muffles sound around the caster, rendering them completely undetectable by mundane mortal senses (Invisibility + Silence effect). Broken by attacking.

"You are not hiding in the shadows. You have become the place where shadows originate."

Coven Circle Amplification [Rite • Level 5]

Requires: 5+ Coven Members • Cost: 2 Blood per caster • Duration: 1 Night

Channels the collective vitae of the coven into a shared focus. Triples the effective range and area of effect of all Hemomancy rites cast within the coven's haven until the next sunrise.

"One thread snaps under tension. Five woven together can hold a city in place."

Blood-Sight Revelation [Rite • Level 1]

Casting Time: 1 Action • Cost: 1 Blood • Duration: 1 Scene

Alters the caster's vision to see the arterial pulse and vital organs of all living beings through obstacles up to 30 ft away. Negates all darkness and concealment penalties against living targets.

"In a room of strangers, the heartbeat is the only honest introduction."

Bone-Splinter Salvo [Rite • Level 3]

Casting Time: 1 Action • Cost: 2 Blood • Range: 40 ft

Forms needle-sharp projectiles of calcified vitae and launches them in a cone. All targets in a 15-ft cone suffer 3 Lethal damage (Dexterity check DC 14 for half).

"The body is already full of weapons. Hemomancy simply reminds them of their purpose."

Tomb-Slumber Preservation [Rite • Level 4]

Casting Time: 1 Hour • Cost: 5 Blood • Duration: Indefinite (until awakened)

Places a willing or helpless vampire into stasis-torpor. The subject does not lose Blood points over time and is completely immune to physical decay, but cannot awaken without external vitae applied to the lips.

"Sleep until the world forgets your name — and wake when it has grown fragile enough to conquer again."

Crimson Feast Benediction [Rite • Level 1]

Casting Time: 1 Action • Cost: 0 Blood • Target: Self

Murmured before feeding from a willing mortal. The mortal suffers no pain and retains only warm, dreamlike memories of the experience, reducing mortal panic checks by 2.

"A predator that leaves its prey grateful will never hunt on an empty stomach."

Pyre-Blood Ignition [Rite • Level 4]

Casting Time: 1 Action • Cost: 3 Blood • Range: 20 ft

Transmutes spilled blood on any surface into alchemical fire. Deals 4 Aggravated (Fire) damage to anyone in contact with the blood and ignites flammable materials within 5 ft.

"You thought the stain on the floor was an accident. It was the fuse."

Ancestral Vitae Communion [Rite • Level 5]

Requires: Ancient Relic • Cost: 5 Blood • Duration: 1 Scene

Opens a psychic conduit to the collective memories of the caster's lineage. Grants temporary mastery of any 1 forgotten Discipline or Ancient Lore skill at rank 4 for the duration of the scene.

"Ten centuries of dead hunters stand behind my shoulder. Do you really think you are the first to try this?"

Blood-Ward of the Five Thresholds [Rite • Level 4]

Casting Time: 30 Minutes • Cost: 4 Blood • Duration: 1 Month

Anoints the five primary entrances of a haven. Mortal intruders suffer Overwhelming Dread (Willpower DC 16 or flee); hostile vampires must expend 2 Blood points per minute of presence inside.

"Five marks upon five lintels. Five reasons to turn back before the door opens."

Flesh-Knit Salve [Rite • Level 2]

Casting Time: 1 Action • Cost: 1 Blood • Range: Touch

Applies charged vitae directly to an ally's wound. Instantly heals 2 points of Lethal or Bashing damage (cannot heal Aggravated damage). Usable once per scene per target.

"The skin closes. The bone sets. Pay the red tax and stand back up."

Bloodline Curse of the Withered Vein [Rite • Level 5]

Requires: Target's True Blood Sample • Cost: 5 Blood • Duration: Permanent (until dispelled)

Inflicts a generational curse upon a target vampire. The target's maximum Blood Pool is permanently reduced by 3, and all healing costs 1 additional Blood point per point healed.

"The wound is not in your flesh. It is written into your line — and every fledgeling you ever sire will inherit the debt."

Crimson Tether of the Thrall-Master [Rite • Level 2]

Casting Time: 10 Minutes • Cost: 1 Blood • Target: Bonded Thrall

Strengthens the psychic link to a bonded thrall. The caster can perceive through the thrall's senses at any time within a 5-mile radius and issue telepathic commands that bypass mortal resistance.

"A good servant anticipates. A bound servant has no choice."

Solar Veil Inscription [Rite • Level 5]

Requires: Obsidian Ink • Cost: 6 Blood • Duration: 1 Day (Sunrise to Sunset)

Tattoos an ancient ward onto the caster's skin. Grants complete immunity to sunlight damage for 1 turn (6 seconds) upon direct exposure, allowing a desperate dash to shelter before immolation begins.

"Six seconds of sunlight. Six seconds to remember what it felt like — and six seconds to run."

Vitae Resonance Tracking [Rite • Level 2]

Casting Time: 1 Action • Cost: 1 Blood • Range: 1 Mile

Tastes a drop of blood and instantly determines the subject's Age Tier, approximate generation, and whether they are currently carrying active supernatural infections or poisons.

"One drop on the tongue. The entire lineage confesses."

Coven Haven Sanctification [Rite • Level 4]

Requires: Coven Council • Cost: 10 Blood (pooled) • Duration: 1 Year

Sanctifies an entire building as a recognized sovereign haven. Supernatural surveillance, scrying, and remote telepathy into the haven fail automatically unless permitted by the master of the haven.

"The walls are stone. The roof is lead. The threshold belongs to the blood."

Final Rite of the Ash-King [Rite • Level 5 • Cataclysmic]

Requires: Ancient Blood (30+ Blood Pool) • Cost: 10 Blood • Duration: 1 Scene

Unleashes a devastating shockwave of boiling vitae across a 50-ft radius. All living and undead creatures within the area suffer 6 Aggravated damage (Stamina soak DC 18 for half). The caster takes 2 un-soakable Lethal damage from recoil.

"When the king burns, the whole kingdom burns with him."
