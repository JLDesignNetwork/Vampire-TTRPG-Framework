# Coven Law Court Protocols & Legal Code v2608.60.0-bs

## Overview & System-Agnostic Adaptation

The **Coven Law Court Protocols & Legal Code** is an official, opt-in supplement for the [Vampire TTRPG Framework](../vampire.md). It codifies the judicial proceedings, tribunal trials, legal dispensations, and punishment enforcement governing vampiric society.

Following [Separation of Concerns](../vampire.md#system-agnostic--multi-genre-framework), this supplement expands upon the core [Social & Coven Laws](../vampire.md#coven-structure--governance). All tribunal rulings consume standard [Coven Governance Laws](../vampire.md#coven-structure--governance) and utilize [Universal Age Tier Superiority Modifiers](../vampire.md#age-tier-superiority).

## <a id="judicial-tribunal-architecture"></a>Judicial Tribunal Architecture

### <a id="presiding-magistrate-and-tribunal-bench"></a>The Presiding Magistrate & Tribunal Bench
Coven judicial trials are presided over by a panel of **3 Elder or Ancient Magistrates**:
* **The High Arbiter:** Presides over trial decorum, reads formal charges, and delivers final sentences.
* **The Sanguine Auditor:** Examines physical evidence, Blood Archive phials, and vitae purity tests.
* **The Keeper of the Shroud:** Evaluates secrecy breaches, mortal exposure risks, and diplomatic impact.

### <a id="evidence-admissibility-and-blood-testimony"></a>Evidence Admissibility & Blood Testimony
Vampiric courts enforce strict evidentiary standards:
* **Blood Archive Phials (`Admissible`):** Uncorrupted memory phials extracted via [*Corpse Siphon*](../vampire.md#corpse-siphon) or consentual blood extraction serve as absolute legal proof.
* **Telepathic Probe Logs (`Conditional`):** Memories extracted via [*Mind Tear*](../vampire.md#mind-tear) require verification by the Sanguine Auditor to rule out cognitive forgery or illusion tampering.
* **Mortal Testimony (`Inadmissible`):** Mundane mortal testimony is legally invalid unless supported by a Thrall blood bond oath or memory phial.

### <a id="four-stages-of-coven-trial-procedure"></a>The Four Stages of Coven Trial Procedure
1. **The Formal Indictment:** The High Arbiter reads the charges alleging a violation of Coven Law ([The Shroud](../vampire.md#the-shroud), [Haven Sanctuary](../vampire.md#law-of-haven-sanctuary), or [Diablerie Taboo](../vampire.md#diablerie-the-ultimate-taboo)).
2. **Evidence Examination:** The Sanguine Auditor inspects memory phials, crime scene soil, or physical remains.
3. **The Blood Oath Defense:** The accused (or their designated Advocate) presents defense arguments, alibis, or [Bloodline Alignment Bonuses](../vampire.md#age-tier-superiority).
4. **Verdict & Sentencing:** The Tribunal Bench casts secret votes using obsidian (guilty) or white quartz (innocent) stones. Sentences are executed immediately.

## <a id="legal-dispensations-and-sanction-protocols"></a>Legal Dispensations & Sanction Protocols

### <a id="sanctioned-termination-protocol"></a>Sanctioned Termination Protocol
Under Coven Law, executing a fellow vampire or a bound Thrall without legal authorization is treated as murder or destruction of Coven property.
* **Execution Authorization:** A Master seeking to terminate an overage Thrall or execute a rogue fledgling must receive a formal **Sanctioned Termination Decree**.
* **Grounds Restriction:** Executions MUST be conducted strictly **outside Coven sanctum grounds** (to prevent desecration of sacred havens and avoid Native Soil contamination).

### <a id="sanctioned-release-dispensation"></a>Sanctioned Release Dispensation
Resolving [Thrall Cap Overage Strain](../vampire.md#thrall-cap) without execution requires a formal **Sanctioned Release Dispensation** signed by the Coven Council:
* **Controlled Euthanasia:** The Thrall is peacefully euthanized via painless alchemical narcotics or sedatives.
* **Memory Erasure & Discharge:** The vampire uses [*Lethe’s Touch*](../vampire.md#lethes-touch) under Tribunal supervision to completely erase all memories of vampiric society, releasing the mortal back into human society with a substantial financial severance.

### <a id="blood-treaty-diplomatic-accords"></a>Blood Treaty Diplomatic Accords
Formal agreements between rival Covens governing hunting perimeters, sanctuary boundaries, and extradition:
* **Sanctuary Immunity:** A vampire seeking asylum inside a neutral Haven cannot be seized while diplomatic negotiations are underway.
* **Extradition Rights:** Covens bound by a Blood Treaty must surrender fleeing lawbreakers within **72 hours** of receiving a formal extradition writ.

## <a id="penalties-and-punishment-codex"></a>Penalties & Punishment Codex

### <a id="tier-1-infractions"></a>Tier 1 Infractions (Sanguine Tithe & Fine)
* **Offenses:** Minor Haven protocol breaches, unauthorized feeding in secondary zones, minor decorum disrespect.
* **Penalty:** Caster must surrender **2 to 5 Blood Reserve phials** into the Coven Vaults or serve 30 days of sanctuary guard duty.

### <a id="tier-2-infractions"></a>Tier 2 Infractions (Enforced Torpor Slumber)
* **Offenses:** Reckless secrecy breaches, assault on a Coven member, unauthorized Thrall creation.
* **Penalty:** The offender is staked through the heart and interred in a sealed stone crypt for a period of **1 to 100 years** of enforced [Torpor Slumber](../vampire.md#stage-4-torpor).

### <a id="tier-3-infractions"></a>Tier 3 Infractions (Tabula Rasa & Sun Fire Execution)
* **Offenses:** Unsanctioned [Diablerie](../vampire.md#diablerie-the-ultimate-taboo), mass Shroud collapse, treasonous collaboration with vampire hunters.
* **Penalty:**
  * **Tabula Rasa:** Complete psychic erasure of all memories, skills, and identity via high-level mental sorcery.
  * **Sun Fire Execution:** The offender is bound to a solar pillar at dawn, suffering total solar combustion down to ash.
