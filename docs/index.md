- **Universal Blood Economy:** Dynamic Vitae pool scaling with generation, feeding frequency, and metabolic expenditure.
- **Gritty Starvation Mechanics:** Tiered penalties, frenzy triggers, and descent into Torpor.
- **System Agnosticism:** Seamlessly adaptable into d20, 2d6, 3d6, or dice-pool resolution systems.
